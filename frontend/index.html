What is ThreatLens?

ThreatLens is an AI-powered platform designed to help identify potentially dangerous emails. It analyzes suspicious emails for phishing patterns, impersonation attempts, spoofed sender information, suspicious links, and other indicators of fraud.

Instead of complicated cybersecurity information, ThreatLens provides clear risk insights and helps you understand whether to trust an email or take precautions.
